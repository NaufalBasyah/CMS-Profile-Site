Scope of business

We make one product family and make it well: E.B.S. from Indonesian palm-based stearic acid, exported to the world's major chemical companies.

How we work

We buy stearic acid from major mills in Indonesia, convert it to E.B.S. through our in-house chemical reaction process, and export HI-LUB to global chemical companies.

Because the whole chain from plantation to stearic acid sits within North Sumatra, our raw material travels a short distance and supply stays steady.

Our raw material

Our main raw material is stearic acid, an oleochemical product three steps downstream from crude palm oil. It is produced by refining and fractionating palm oil and palm kernel oil.
