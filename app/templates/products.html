HI-LUB ethylene bis stearamide

A palm-based wax amide that lubricates, releases and disperses in thermoplastics without affecting their chemical stability or surface appearance.

What HI-LUB is

HI-LUB is ethylene bis stearamide (E.B.S.), made by reacting stearic acid with ethylenediamine. It is insoluble in water, alcohol, aliphatics and aromatics at room temperature, so it can be added to thermoplastics without reducing their chemical stability.

It has excellent heat stability and practically no moisture or ash. Its high melting point makes it a standard choice for forming lubricating films that hold up through demanding processing.

How it works

HI-LUB acts as both an internal and a surface lubricant. Inside the resin it lowers the power needed for mixing and extrusion and improves flow through dies and into moulds. It also disperses pigment, for faster blending and more even colour.

Milled into ABS or rigid PVC, it gives excellent lubricity without spoiling the finished part. Its limited compatibility with polystyrene, ABS, polyethylene, PVC, nylon, cellulose acetate and polyvinyl acetate is what makes it work well as a lubricant.
